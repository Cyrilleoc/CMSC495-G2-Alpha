## Suggested 250-word structure (write from actual evidence)

- **45 words:** Review context, module, review criteria, and team roles.
- **80 words:** Concrete code-quality, security, performance, integration, and documentation findings.
- **65 words:** Changes actually made because of feedback, with tests and commit references.
- **45 words:** Two distinct technical-debt instances, their impact, and realistic mitigations.
- **15 words:** Outcome, remaining limitations and next review step.

Do not claim work, merged PRs, AI calls, security remediation, or CI results that did not actually happen.
